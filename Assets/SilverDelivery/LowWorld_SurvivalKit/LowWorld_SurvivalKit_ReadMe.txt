Low World - Survival Kit

Low World survival game kit with a "low poly" look.
Contains 123 assets, everything from modular buildings to weapons to props such as medicine, food, furniture and much more.

CONTENTS:
118 meshes
12 wooden modular building parts (avg. 732 faces per model)
12 plank modular building parts (avg. 874 faces per model)
12 stone modular building parts (avg. 2023 faces per model)
25 food models (avg. 289 faces per model)
11 furniture models (avg. 848 faces per model)
5 medicine models (avg. 299 faces per model)
24 prop models (avg. 242 faces per model)
12 melee weapon models (avg. 382 faces per model) 
5 ranged weapon models (avg. 839 faces per model)
4 ammunition models (avg. 267 faces per model)

4 skinned meshes
pistol (1349 faces)
revolver (758 faces)
rifle (491 faces)
shotgun (953 faces)

123 icon textures (512 x 512px)
one for each item

2 materials
m_lowworld_survivalkit
m_lowworld_survivalkit_emmission (for the flashlight model)

1 texture (64 x 64px)
t_lowworld_survivalkit_albedo (for all models)

HOW TO USE:
1. Go to the "prefabs" folder, drag and drop... good luck :).
2. *Optional* In the "materials" folder customize the colors of the materials using the material interface.
